# Product Workflow

## Philosophy

Kendrix is built around a guided storytelling workflow.

Each stage prepares the next stage.

Creators make creative decisions.

Kendrix handles production.

---

## Workflow

### 1. Create Channel

Every project belongs to a channel.

The channel defines long-term creative consistency.

Examples:

* Horror
* Children's Stories
* History
* Documentary
* Educational

The channel establishes identity, audience, and recurring creative direction.

---

### 2. Creative Planning

Define:

* story idea
* audience
* genre
* desired emotion
* publishing platform
* content objective

Output:

A clear creative brief.

Most of the brief is inherited from the channel. Per-story planning adds only what is new — usually the idea itself.

---

### 3. Story Development

Generate the story.

Review.

Edit.

Refine.

Approve.

The story should be considered the source of truth for every downstream stage.

---

### 4. Storyboard

Break the story into scenes.

Each scene should define:

* purpose
* narration
* visual intent
* emotional objective

Storyboard review is encouraged before production begins.

---

### 5. Production

Generate:

* visuals
* narration
* music
* sound effects
* subtitles

Every generated asset should remain editable where practical.

---

### 6. Assembly

Combine assets into the final video.

Ensure synchronization between:

* visuals
* narration
* captions
* music
* sound design

---

### 7. Review

Review the complete production.

Look for:

* pacing
* continuity
* synchronization
* emotional impact
* technical quality

Creators should feel confident before publishing.

---

### 8. Publish

Export or publish to supported platforms.

Publishing should be the final step—not another production phase.

---

## Review Is Graduated

Every review point in this workflow is an option, not a tollgate.

The workflow must be able to run end-to-end with zero manual review — Kendrix's own channels run this way. No stage may block waiting for a human by default.

Creators choose their level of involvement per channel: review everything, review by sampling, or trust the workflow and handle exceptions.

The purpose of quality work is to make review unnecessary. When the same manual correction recurs, the fix belongs in the engine, not in the review habit.

---

## Guiding Principles

Every stage should answer:

* What decision is the creator making?
* What work is Kendrix automating?
* What information should carry into the next stage?

Avoid unnecessary repetition.

Avoid forcing creators to repeat information already known.
