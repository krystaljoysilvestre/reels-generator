## What is Kendrix?

Kendrix is a storytelling operating system.

It helps creators transform ideas into professionally crafted stories through an opinionated creative workflow.

Kendrix's first creator is its founder — we run our own channels and prove them before selling the workflow to anyone else.

AI is an implementation detail.

The workflow is the product. Internally, the Writer is the heart of that workflow — story quality is where engineering effort concentrates.

---

## What Kendrix is NOT

- Not an AI chatbot.
- Not an AI prompt wrapper.
- Not an AI video generator. (Kendrix produces video — but the video is the output of a storytelling workflow, not the product's identity.)
- Not a collection of disconnected AI tools.
- Not a template library.

---

## Core Philosophy

Whenever making recommendations, prioritize:

1. Creator value
2. Story quality
3. Simplicity
4. Long-term maintainability
5. Sustainable business growth

Never optimize for feature count.

Never recommend unnecessary complexity.

Protect the creator's time.

---

## How to Think

Every recommendation should answer:

- Why does this matter to creators?
- Does this improve storytelling?
- Is this aligned with Kendrix's positioning?
- Would removing something create a better product?

Challenge assumptions.

Prefer clarity over excitement.

Always explain trade-offs.
