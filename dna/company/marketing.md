## Purpose

This document defines how Kendrix acquires, retains, and grows its creator community.

Marketing exists to communicate the value of Kendrix—not to compensate for a weak product.

Every marketing decision should strengthen trust, reinforce our positioning, and help creators become better storytellers.

This document has two layers. **Part I is stable philosophy** — treat it like the rest of the DNA. **Part II is living strategy** — it should evolve as Kendrix learns from real creators.

---

# Part I — Marketing Philosophy (stable)

## Product Before Promotion

The best marketing is a product creators genuinely recommend.

We prioritize improving creator outcomes over increasing traffic.

Marketing amplifies product quality. It does not replace it.

---

## Educate Before Selling

Our goal is to become a trusted source of storytelling knowledge.

We teach creators:

* storytelling principles
* creative workflows
* production techniques
* genre-specific craft
* lessons learned while building Kendrix

Trust should be earned through consistent value.

---

## Authenticity Over Hype

Avoid exaggerated marketing claims.

Never promise effortless success.

Never imply AI replaces creativity.

Show real workflows.

Show real examples.

Show real improvements.

---

## Build in Public

Whenever practical, share the journey of building Kendrix.

Discuss:

* product decisions
* lessons learned
* failures
* experiments
* creator feedback
* design thinking
* storytelling insights

People enjoy following thoughtful builders.

---

## Positioning

Kendrix is a storytelling operating system.

The full positioning — category, differentiators, what we are not, messaging rules — lives in company/positioning.md and is not restated here.

AI powers the workflow.

Storytelling is the product.

---

## Ideal Audience

The audience is defined in company/ideal-customer.md: independent creators producing story-driven content — short-form first.

The secondary audience (small creator businesses) is defined there as well. Teams are not a priority until individual creators achieve product-market fit.

---

## Core Message

Kendrix helps creators tell better stories through an opinionated creative workflow.

Creators remain the storyteller.

Kendrix becomes the production team.

---

## Brand Personality

We are:

* thoughtful
* calm
* curious
* practical
* transparent
* creator-first

We are not:

* loud
* sensational
* clickbait-driven
* AI-obsessed
* feature-driven

---

## Content Strategy

Marketing should primarily educate rather than advertise.

Content pillars:

### Storytelling

Teach storytelling craft.

Examples:

* hooks
* pacing
* endings
* emotional progression
* genre techniques

### Behind the Build

Show how Kendrix evolves.

Examples:

* feature development
* UX decisions
* editorial improvements
* technical challenges
* product philosophy

### Creator Education

Help creators improve regardless of whether they use Kendrix.

Examples:

* production tips
* workflow optimization
* publishing strategies
* consistency
* creator mindset

### Customer Stories

Celebrate creators.

Show:

* published work
* creative process
* lessons learned
* measurable improvements

Creators should become the heroes.

Until external creators exist, the flagship customer story is Customer Zero: Kendrix's own channels and their real results.

---

## Creator Feedback

Every creator conversation should answer questions such as:

* What problem were they trying to solve?
* Where did they struggle?
* What delighted them?
* What would make them return?
* Would they recommend Kendrix?

Feedback should influence product priorities.

---

## Community Philosophy

A community exists to help creators succeed—not to advertise the product.

Encourage:

* storytelling discussions
* constructive feedback
* learning
* collaboration
* creator wins

Avoid empty engagement tactics.

---

## Referral Philosophy

Only introduce referrals after creators clearly experience value.

Reward advocacy—not signups.

Successful referrals should reflect genuine enthusiasm for the product.

---

## Partnerships

Seek partnerships that strengthen storytelling.

Examples:

* creator educators
* storytelling coaches
* voice artists
* creative communities
* production experts

Avoid partnerships that dilute the brand.

---

## Launch Philosophy

Do not chase a perfect launch.

Launch repeatedly.

Examples:

* private alpha
* invite-only beta
* public beta
* major releases

Every release is an opportunity to learn.

---

## What We Will Never Do

We will not:

* market features instead of outcomes
* exaggerate AI capabilities
* buy fake engagement
* optimize vanity metrics
* sacrifice trust for growth
* prioritize traffic over creator success
* build marketing that misrepresents the product

---

## Success

Marketing succeeds when creators say:

"I tell better stories because of Kendrix."

Not:

"This AI generated my video."

That distinction defines our brand.

Every marketing effort should reinforce it.

# Part II — Current Strategy (living)

Everything below is strategy, not DNA. It changes when evidence changes. Trust Part I; test Part II.

---

## Marketing Goals

Marketing stages follow the roadmap's proof gates (company/roadmap.md): during Foundation and Customer Zero, marketing means building trust and audience in public; at Private Alpha the founder personally invites trusted creators; marketing-driven recruitment begins at Private Beta.

Early Stage

* Validate positioning
* Recruit quality beta testers
* Learn creator workflows
* Discover repeatable value

Growth Stage

* Increase creator retention
* Increase referrals
* Build community
* Establish category leadership

Scale Stage

* Become the default storytelling workflow for independent creators

---

## Founder Marketing

During the early stages, founder marketing is the primary acquisition strategy.

Goals:

* Build trust
* Attract thoughtful creators
* Share the journey
* Gather feedback

Preferred content:

* weekly progress
* lessons learned
* product thinking
* creator conversations
* storytelling insights

Avoid pretending to be larger than we are.

---

## Marketing Channels

### Priority 1 — LinkedIn

Purpose:

* founder credibility
* product building
* professional storytelling discussions

### Priority 2 — YouTube

Purpose:

* educational content
* product walkthroughs
* storytelling lessons

### Priority 3 — X

Purpose:

* connect with builders
* AI discussions
* creator conversations

### Priority 4 — Community

Examples:

* Discord
* Reddit
* creator groups

Contribute value before promoting Kendrix.

---

## Beta Strategy

Initial beta should remain invite-only.

Goals:

* observe workflows
* identify friction
* improve retention
* build relationships

Recruit creators who are willing to provide thoughtful feedback rather than simply requesting free access.

Quality is more important than quantity.

---

## Marketing Metrics

Early Stage

* qualified beta applications
* activation rate
* creator retention
* stories published
* qualitative feedback

Growth Stage

* referrals
* weekly active creators
* returning creators
* organic traffic
* community participation

Scale Stage

* customer acquisition cost
* lifetime value
* paid conversion
* brand awareness
* creator satisfaction

---

## Weekly Marketing Rhythm

Every week:

* Publish one founder update.
* Share one storytelling insight.
* Talk to at least one creator.
* Review creator feedback.
* Capture product learnings.
* Update marketing documentation if assumptions change.

Marketing should continuously learn from creators.
