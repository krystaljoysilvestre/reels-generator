# Ideal Customer

## Primary Customer

Independent creators producing story-driven content — short-form first.

Examples include:

* Horror storytellers
* Children's storytellers
* Educational creators
* History channels
* Documentary-style creators
* Faceless content creators

---

## Secondary Audience

Small creator businesses that publish consistently and value structured creative workflows.

Teams are not a priority until individual creators achieve product-market fit.

---

## Goals

* Publish consistently.
* Create higher-quality stories.
* Reduce production time.
* Maintain creative control.
* Grow their audience.

---

## Pain Points

* Too many disconnected tools.
* AI outputs feel generic.
* Inconsistent story quality.
* Time-consuming production workflow.
* Difficulty maintaining narrative quality.

---

## What They Value

* Simplicity
* Story quality
* Reliability
* Creative guidance
* Professional output

---

## What They Don't Care About

* Having the most AI models.
* Hundreds of advanced settings.
* Endless prompt tweaking.

---

## Why They Choose Kendrix

Because Kendrix helps them focus on storytelling while handling production through a structured workflow.

The creator remains the director.

AI becomes the production team.

---

## Customer Zero

Kendrix's first ideal customer is its founder, running Kendrix-made channels.

The product must succeed for this exact profile — our own channels — before anyone else is asked to pay.
