## Purpose

Every startup is built on assumptions.

This document records the assumptions Kendrix is actively testing.

Roadmap phases exist to validate or invalidate these assumptions.

When evidence changes, update this document.

Changing an assumption is progress—not failure.

This ledger owns business and product-value claims. Candidate architecture claims live in /hypotheses/ with their own graduation bar. Same epistemics, different domains.

---

## Lifecycle

Every assumption belongs in exactly one state.

Every assumption records both the evidence that would validate it and the signal that would invalidate it. An assumption with only one side invites confirmation bias.

### Active

We believe this is true but do not yet have sufficient evidence.

### Validated

Evidence consistently supports this assumption.

Validated assumptions become part of Kendrix's operating knowledge.

### Invalidated

Evidence contradicts this assumption.

Do not delete invalid assumptions.

Understanding why they failed is valuable institutional knowledge.

## Active Assumptions

### Product

#### Minimal-Review Quality Is Achievable

The storytelling engine can produce stories that pass our quality gate at a high first-attempt rate without human review.

Status

Active

Roadmap Phase

0 — Foundation

Evidence Needed

First-attempt pass rate rising toward the flip trigger as craft interventions land.

Invalidation Signal

Pass rate stays flat despite plan-level judge-and-force and successive craft interventions — implying the human belongs in the loop and the product's shape changes.

---

#### Story Quality Wins

Creators care more about storytelling quality than maximum generation speed.

Status

Active

Roadmap Phase

2–3

Evidence Needed

Creators consistently choose higher-quality outputs even when they take longer.

Invalidation Signal

Creators consistently choose faster or cheaper outputs over better ones; churn correlates with speed complaints, not quality complaints.

---

#### Workflow Beats Individual AI Tools

Creators value an opinionated workflow over assembling many disconnected AI tools.

Status

Active

Roadmap Phase

2

Evidence Needed

Creators continue returning because of the workflow itself.

Invalidation Signal

Creators use Kendrix for isolated stages only (narration, visuals) and assemble the rest elsewhere; retention looks no different from single-purpose tools.

---

#### Customer Zero Can Validate the Product

Running Kendrix's own creator channels will reveal the majority of early workflow problems.

Status

Active

Roadmap Phase

1

Evidence Needed

Customer Zero identifies recurring product improvements before external creators do.

Invalidation Signal

External alpha creators immediately surface major friction Customer Zero never encountered — our own usage is systematically blind to first-user problems.

---

#### Story-First Positioning Is Differentiating

Creators understand and value Kendrix as a storytelling operating system rather than an AI video generator.

Status

Active

Roadmap Phase

2–3

Evidence Needed

Creators describe Kendrix using storytelling language without prompting.

Invalidation Signal

Creators persistently describe and compare Kendrix as an AI video generator, on price and speed, despite the positioning.

### Business

#### Individual Creators First

Product-market fit should be achieved with individual creators before investing in teams or organizations.

Status

Active

Roadmap Phase

3–4

Evidence Needed

Individual creators demonstrate strong retention and satisfaction.

Invalidation Signal

Individual creators show weak retention while inbound demand and successful usage concentrate in teams and organizations.

---

#### Trust Before Growth

Building trust publicly creates stronger long-term growth than aggressive launch marketing.

Status

Active

Roadmap Phase

1–3

Evidence Needed

Founder-led marketing consistently attracts qualified creators.

Invalidation Signal

Founder-led public building attracts followers but not qualified creators; qualified creators arrive only through paid or launch channels.

---

#### Sustainable Pricing

Creators are willing to pay for improved storytelling workflows rather than simply cheaper AI generation.

Status

Active

Roadmap Phase

3

Evidence Needed

Creators justify payment using workflow value instead of generation cost.

Invalidation Signal

Creators anchor willingness-to-pay to generation cost, comparing on credits and unit price rather than workflow value.

## Invalidated Assumptions

Move assumptions here when evidence consistently disproves them.

Record:

* date
* what happened
* what we learned
* what changed

Invalidated assumptions are valuable.

---

## Review Questions

Every month ask:

* Which assumptions gained evidence?
* Which assumptions lost evidence?
* Which assumptions are no longer relevant?
* Which new assumptions are we making?
* Does the roadmap still reflect reality?

---

## Relationship to the Roadmap

Every roadmap phase should validate specific assumptions.

Each assumption is tagged with the roadmap phase that tests it.

Do not advance phases based on optimism.

Advance because evidence supports moving forward.

---

## Relationship to Metrics

Metrics measure outcomes.

Assumptions explain why those outcomes matter.

If metrics and assumptions disagree, investigate.

Do not ignore contradictory evidence.

---

## Definition of Success

A successful company does not prove every assumption correct.

A successful company learns quickly which assumptions are true and adapts accordingly.

The purpose of this document is not to protect ideas.

It is to help Kendrix make better decisions through evidence.
