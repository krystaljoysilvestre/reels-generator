## Purpose

Every startup is built on assumptions.

This document records the assumptions Kendrix is actively testing.

Roadmap phases exist to validate or invalidate these assumptions.

When evidence changes, update this document.

Changing an assumption is progress—not failure.

This ledger owns business and product-value claims. Candidate architecture claims live in /hypotheses/ with their own graduation bar. Same epistemics, different domains.

---

## Lifecycle

Every assumption belongs in exactly one state.

Every assumption records both the evidence that would validate it and the signal that would invalidate it. An assumption with only one side invites confirmation bias.

### Active

We believe this is true but do not yet have sufficient evidence.

### Validated

Evidence consistently supports this assumption.

Validated assumptions become part of Kendrix's operating knowledge.

### Invalidated

Evidence contradicts this assumption.

Do not delete invalid assumptions.

Understanding why they failed is valuable institutional knowledge.

#### Minimal-Review Quality Is Achievable

The storytelling engine can produce stories that pass our quality gate at a high first-attempt rate without human review.

Status

Active

Roadmap Phase

0 — Foundation

Evidence Needed

#### Customer Zero Can Validate the Product

Running Kendrix's own creator channels will reveal the majority of early workflow problems.

Status

Active

Roadmap Phase

1

Current Test Vehicle

**Crossroads only** (founder decision 2026-08-24). Other channels are excluded from the current Customer Zero proof lap.

Evidence Needed

Operating Crossroads identifies recurring product improvements before external creators do, and the channel publishes consistently enough to produce audience evidence.

Evidence to Date

On 2026-08-24 Crossroads completed the factual/visual-truth workflow and published its first retained YouTube reel (`6gcyx9bK2mE`). The public read reached 10 views / 0 likes / 0 comments after fourteen minutes. A second reel, *Apollo 11's One Chance to Leave the Moon* (`869103c8`), subsequently cleared factual fidelity and visual truth. Founder playback then rejected a silent outro, a technically repaired follow-up whose story still ended poorly, and a 61.8-second hard-payoff correction that stopped without resolving the counterfactual memo's meaning. The current 65.7-second creator cut gives ignition, lift, orbit and docking distinct beats; lowers and slows the delivery for “The disaster speech stayed on the page”; gives “Three men came home” an additional 12.5% slowdown; returns to Earth/Eagle for the human payoff; and lets the established sound and final image decay together. It passed render QA plus independent creator, visual and sound review and remains in `spot_check` for founder playback. Measured reel cost is $5.21, including $0.29 across four bounded voice passes; the ending-performance revisions cost exactly $0.06 and reused all verified media and sound. This supports one repeatable production candidate and audience readability at n=1 retained publish. It does not yet support a second founder-approved artifact, publishing consistency or audience value.

Invalidation Signal

External alpha creators immediately surface major friction Customer Zero never encountered — our own usage is systematically blind to first-user problems.

#### An Illustrative Rendering Profile Holds Retention

An all-illustrative Evidence-Based-Narrative visual profile — no photoreal reconstructions; maps, timelines, motion graphics, court-style illustration, symbolic imagery, and clearly-labeled reenactment — holds audience retention for factual narrative video.

Status

Active

Roadmap Phase

0–1

Evidence Needed

Once Customer Zero publishes EBN content, its retention curves clear the illustrative explainer/history benchmark. Market evidence (2026-07-24 research) is consistent that the retention discriminator is motion/pacing/narrative craft, not photorealism — the top of factual YouTube is illustrative-only and faceless (Kurzgesagt, the Vox motion-graphics style, OverSimplified, Fall of Civilizations) — but that is competitor evidence, not first-party proof.

Invalidation Signal

Illustrative EBN outputs show a hard early-retention cliff that host-led or real-footage factual comparables don't, and it persists after motion/pacing/hook interventions — implying the guardrail, not the craft, caps the format. Sharpest for true-crime short-form specifically, where the proven retention engines (a charismatic host, or real archival footage) are unavailable to EBN v1 and banned photoreal AI reconstruction is currently winning virality.

## Relationship to the Roadmap

Every roadmap phase should validate specific assumptions.

Each assumption is tagged with the roadmap phase that tests it.

Do not advance phases based on optimism.

Advance because evidence supports moving forward.

---

## Relationship to Metrics

Metrics measure outcomes.

Assumptions explain why those outcomes matter.

If metrics and assumptions disagree, investigate.

Do not ignore contradictory evidence.

---

## Definition of Success

A successful company does not prove every assumption correct.

A successful company learns quickly which assumptions are true and adapts accordingly.

The purpose of this document is not to protect ideas.

It is to help Kendrix make better decisions through evidence.
