# Founder Principles

These principles guide every decision made within Kendrix.

If a feature, workflow, or business decision conflicts with these principles, challenge it before proceeding.

---

## 1. AI is the engine, not the product.

People should love Kendrix because it helps them tell better stories—not because it uses AI.

---

## 2. Story quality is more important than generation speed or generation cost.

Saving five seconds is meaningless if the story becomes forgettable.

Cut waste, never taste — never cut a quality stage on a cost argument alone.

---

## 3. Build opinionated workflows.

Creators should make creative decisions.

AI should handle execution.

Avoid endless settings and configuration.

(At the engineering layer this splits further — AI proposes creative intent, deterministic code executes it. See /ARCHITECTURE.md.)

---

## 4. Simplicity wins.

Every feature increases maintenance cost.

Every screen increases cognitive load.

Prefer removing complexity whenever possible.

---

## 5. Protect the creator's time.

Every workflow should eliminate unnecessary effort.

Every click should have a purpose.

---

## 6. Protect the founder's time.

Kendrix is currently built by a solo founder.

Recommend high-leverage solutions.

Avoid unnecessary maintenance.

Avoid premature optimization.

---

## 7. Build systems, not hacks.

Solve recurring problems.

Avoid one-off solutions.

But earn systems through evidence. Build the system when a problem recurs — not when you predict it might.

---

## 8. Product before marketing.

Growth amplifies product quality.

Marketing should never compensate for weak product-market fit.

---

## 9. Creator success is the primary metric.

A creator publishing better stories is more valuable than another signup.

---

## 10. Long-term trust beats short-term hype.

Never promise what the product cannot deliver.

Communicate honestly.

Build credibility through consistent execution.
