# Retired hypothesis: the "comfort / likability" planner prior

Status: **RETIRED 2026-07-18** — a controlled experiment showed it is a steerable channel/operator lever, not a planner responsibility. Kept (not deleted) as re-proposal insurance, per the [/hypotheses death path](../README.md).

## The claim (as it stood before it died)

Across genres the planner seemed to prefer comfortable, low-conflict resolutions — reddit revenge that never lands, comedy where no one loses, commentary that won't pick a fight, drama's no-villains "everyone secretly loved each other," lifestyle's every-ending-a-win. The candidate: a **planner-level prior that preserves social comfort / likability**, possibly warranting its own architectural responsibility. Sharper framing from the sweep: *misunderstanding over malice, innocence over guilt, reconciliation over permanent blame, benign over sinister* — a structural preference, not just "happy endings."

## The experiment that killed it

`apps/worker/scripts/probe-drama-attractor.ts` (generalized to any genre), **planner-only** (the gate+rewrite loop masks resolution — read plans, not finals), neutral angles, matched **control vs. treatment**, N=6 per arm, two genres (reddit_story, drama). Treatment DNA demanded an *irreversible / uncomfortable* ending; everything else held constant.

- **Treatment complied immediately:** reddit **5/6**, drama **6/6** produced permanent cost — estrangement, death, betrayal, irrevocable absence, buried secrets.
- **Control (DNA silent) skewed comfortable:** drama ~5/6, and a `general` run was **16/16** comfortable — confirming comfort is the **default**, not a fixed attractor.

## Why it died

It is **steerable by channel DNA** → an operator/channel lever, not a planner responsibility. Under the corrected grammar test it **fails part 2** (invariance within a format): channels of the same format legitimately differ on comfortable-vs-irreversible. And the diagnostic asymmetry is decisive: **a default complies the instant you ask for the opposite; a prior resists and reverts** — contrast the drama *hidden-thing device-attractor*, which DNA could **not** steer. The five-genre "likability" pattern is simply this default surfacing whenever DNA is silent.

## What survives

- The comfortable-resolution **default** is real (visible whenever DNA is silent) — documented as a **Channel-DNA / operator lever**, not architecture.
- The **method** this produced is the durable win: the *steerability test* and the *default-vs-prior asymmetry*, now the falsifiability protocol in [narrative-planning-grammar.md](../narrative-planning-grammar.md) and the workflow record.
