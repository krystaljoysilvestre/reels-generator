Tawa lahat, sabog na sabog! Sabi ng kakampi ko, 'Naku, bagong tradition 'to!' Masaya kami — kasi kahit nagkaguluhan ng tricks and tactics, mas memorable pa rin yung buong laro. May good sportsmanship pa rin, kahit pabiro lang.

So ayun — every holiday game from now on, may parol! At least hindi na ulit siya magiging coach ng kalaban. Okay na next year!
